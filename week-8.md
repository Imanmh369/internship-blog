# Week 8 – Getting Started with Mathaf Archives Project

## Sunday
On Sunday, my colleague Jillian and I began exploring face recognition. We started by watching tutorial videos and trying to understand the core concepts. After some time, we discovered **InsightFace**, an open-source platform specializing in 2D and 3D facial recognition. Excited by its potential, we decided to dive in and set up a GitHub repository so we could collaborate and build together.

## Monday
On Monday, we gave a demo of the robots to the Mathaf staff. During the session, we showcased the robots by having them perform dances and invited the staff to share ideas for possible use cases. We also inserted SIM cards into both robots to enable network connectivity and checked to ensure everything was working properly.

## Tuesday
On Tuesday, my colleague Jillian and I continued working on face recognition. We began coding with **InsightFace** and downloaded the images provided to us. Next, we extracted embeddings from each image and tested them to ensure everything was functioning as expected. Once we confirmed the results, we moved forward with developing our project.

## Wednesday
On Wednesday, we worked on speech-to-text recognition. For this, we used **ElevenLabs**, which allowed us to extract audio and convert it into speech. We then began building an interface to showcase our work, integrating matched images into the design. Although the core functionality was in place, we still needed to refine the interface and improve some features to make it more user-friendly.

## Thursday
On Thursday, we held a demo for the end user, where we presented our work and explained the process. After the presentation, we received valuable feedback, and Jillian and I immediately decided to start working on the suggested improvements.



## Reflection
This week was full of hands-on learning and collaboration. From experimenting with face recognition and speech-to-text to showcasing our robots, each day brought new challenges and opportunities. The demos and feedback sessions helped us see the practical impact of our work and guided us toward refining our projects further.
