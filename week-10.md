# Getting started with Mathaf Archives project

## Sunday
I focused on refining the search archive to support filtering by genre and location, followed by troubleshooting the transcript features. After discovering that the download feature was not working, I worked on implementing an edit transcript option and fixing the timestamp functionality. These efforts led into a productive discussion with my colleague regarding our upcoming feature priorities.

## Monday
I focused on enhancing our cluster management system by quantifying the data to dynamically display the exact count of both named and unnamed clusters. I then implemented functionality that allows items to be seamlessly moved between clusters. Additionally, I refined the user interface for the verified and unverified status filters to make data sorting more intuitive.

To conclude the day, my colleague suggested utilizing a Raspberry Pi for deploying our web application, and we officially aligned on this hardware approach as a key objective for the next sprint.

## Tuesday
My colleague Jillian and I collaborated on the deployment process. We transferred the repository to the Raspberry Pi, pulled the required container images, and installed all necessary packages. After troubleshooting several configuration issues and iterating through a number of fixes, we successfully deployed the Mathaf Archive to the server.

Following the successful deployment, I began development of the new batch editing feature.

## Wednesday
I focused on advancing the batch editing workflow and ultimately decided to streamline the user experience by redesigning the layout to combine image movement and batch editing into a single unified feature.

Afterward, I shifted my attention to implementing Excel export functionality organized by binders. I began working on the user interface design while simultaneously monitoring the Raspberry Pi's performance to ensure stable operation during active feature usage.

## Thursday
My colleague and I met with Rachelle and other curators from Mathaf to collaborate on artist recognition within our data clusters. During the meeting, we reviewed upcoming features, discussed potential improvements, and gathered valuable feedback on design updates and user interface enhancements.

## Reflection
Reflecting on this week, I am proud of the steady momentum we maintained across both technical development and team collaboration. We successfully progressed from complex cluster management improvements and user interface refinements to deploying the Mathaf Archive on a Raspberry Pi server. The week also included meaningful stakeholder engagement, helping us align future development priorities while continuing to improve the overall usability and functionality of the platform.
