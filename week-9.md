# Week 9 – Getting Started with Mathaf Archives Project

## Sunday
On Sunday, my colleague Jillian and I continued developing the Mathaf archive project, building on the feedback we received during last week’s review. I focused on refining the dataset so that each cluster could be clearly labeled. To achieve this, I ensured that every cluster was tagged with the corresponding artist’s name. After some back and forth, we agreed that using the artist’s name as a primary identifier was the most effective solution.  
With the labeling system established, I then began exploring similarity measures. This feature introduces an accuracy percentage for each match, allowing users to see how reliable the image comparison is.

## Monday
On Monday, I completed the similarity feature, ensuring that each matched image displays an accuracy percentage so users can clearly understand the reliability of the results. After that, I moved on to enhancing the interface by adding captions to every image card, giving users immediate context about what each card represents. I also implemented a rewrite feature for naming the artist.  
Later in the day, Jillian and I had an update meeting with our supervisor, where we presented our progress and received constructive feedback on areas for improvement. Following the meeting, we discussed the changes to be made and divided the tasks between us to keep the project moving forward efficiently.

## Tuesday
On Tuesday, I began working on the search archive feature. We first discussed the idea together, then revisited the dataset, which had been updated by my colleague. Because of those changes, I needed to make adjustments to the search archive to ensure it aligned correctly with the new data structure.  
After refining the feature, we talked through the next steps and outlined what each of us would focus on moving forward.

## Wednesday
On Wednesday, I worked on exporting the files and successfully completed that step. While reviewing the output, I noticed that the route was inconsistent between the tag page and the cluster face. After the routing issue was resolved, I went back and made small refinements to the Excel sheet to keep the structure clean and consistent.

## Thursday
On Thursday, we continued refining the project and later met with Rachelle to review the progress we had made over the week. During the meeting, we received valuable feedback, including suggestions to add a batch editing feature, quantify the list, and enable editing within clusters.  
After discussing how to approach these changes and dividing responsibilities, I went back to refine the similarity feature once more to accommodate the latest data updates. These adjustments are helping us steadily improve the archive’s functionality and prepare it for more advanced user interactions.



## Reflection
This week’s work on the Mathaf archive project showed how important it is to stay flexible with evolving data and feedback. From refining similarity measures to adding captions, rewriting artist names, and adjusting routes, each change pushed the project toward accuracy and usability.
